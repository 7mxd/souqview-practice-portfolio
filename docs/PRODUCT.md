# Product

## Register
Product. The design serves a task: add a practice trade, then see how the portfolio is doing.

## Users and purpose
First-time investors in the UAE, mostly students, practising with pretend money before they invest for real. They often use it on a phone, in short sessions. The main job: add a few trades and understand gain or loss in AED.

## Personality
Calm, clear, trustworthy. Warm sand-and-gold palette with green for gains and deep red for losses. It should feel like a careful learning tool, not a trading terminal.

## Anti-references
- SouqView's own name, logo or wordmark. This is a hiring task, not a SouqView product.
- Trading-app clichés: tickers flashing red and green, rockets, bulls and bears, crypto glow.
- Decorative motion and page-load choreography.

## Constraints
- The brief's five fixed sample prices, with everything shown in AED at 3.6725 per dollar.
- WCAG 2.2 AA, checked by `e2e/a11y.mjs` in CI. Touch targets at least 44 px.
- Motion only to show state or give feedback, 150–250 ms, with `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
